Who We Are

Built for the complexity
of Nigerian markets

GreenScope Data Lab is a Nigerian primary research and market intelligence firm. We exist because the decisions that shape businesses, policy, and livelihoods in Nigeria deserve to be grounded in real field data — not assumptions, not extrapolations from other markets.

We work across urban markets, peri-urban communities, and rural settlements, deploying trained field teams with digital tools to capture what is actually happening at the consumer and retail level — in the north, the south, and everywhere in between.

Our clients include FMCG companies tracking brand health, diaspora and institutional investors evaluating Nigerian market opportunities, NGOs and development partners running M&E programs, and government agencies designing evidence-based programs.

Geographic Reach

North. South. Everywhere
decisions are made.

Most Nigerian research firms default to Lagos. GreenScope is built differently. Our founding team has deep operational roots in northern Nigeria — Kaduna, Kano, Sokoto, Abuja — with the retailer networks and community access that most firms lack outside the south.

Our Wave 1 fieldwork covers Kaduna (Kaduna Central Market, Kawo Market, Narayi/Sabo) and Anambra (Onitsha Main Market, Eke Awka, Eke Awkuzu) — giving clients a genuine North vs. South consumer intelligence comparison, not just a Lagos-centric view.

Subsequent waves will expand to Kano, Port Harcourt, Abuja, Ibadan, and beyond.
